Service Delivery Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

College Kundli sells digital services only. No physical products are shipped, and there are no shipping charges.

How each service is delivered

  • Call Wallet recharge: credited to your in-app wallet immediately after the payment is confirmed, usually within seconds.
  • Paid calls: delivered as live voice calls inside the app, connected as soon as the guide accepts.
  • Booked consultations: delivered as an in-app session with the guide you booked, starting once payment is confirmed and the guide joins.

If a paid service is not delivered, our Refund & Cancellation Policy applies. For help, email support@collegekundli.in.
